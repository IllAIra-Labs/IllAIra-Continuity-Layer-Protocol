# Download — App CLM (Android)

**[illaira.com/download](https://illaira.com/download)** — public beta `0.1.69`, sideload APK.

* ~183 MB, `arm64-v8a` + `x86_64`, Android 7.0 or later. Will not install on 32-bit devices.
* Sideload only — not on Google Play. Signed with an IllAIra Labs developer key, so Android will warn
  you before installing; that warning is about the file's source and we can't remove it from our side.
* Version, SHA-256 and size for every build are published at
  [`illaira.com/app/latest.json`](https://illaira.com/app/latest.json) — check what you downloaded
  against what we say we published.
* Prefer the browser, or on iPhone? Use the CLM WebApp instead: [illaira.com/webapp](https://illaira.com/webapp).
* Found a bug in the beta? [illaira.com/contatti](https://illaira.com/contatti).

See [README.md](./README.md) for what the app does and [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md) for what shipped and when.
